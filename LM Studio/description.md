**Useful Local AI Agent Tool — LM Studio**

[LM Studio](https://lmstudio.ai/?utm_source=chatgpt.com) is a powerful local AI application that allows you to download, manage, and run large language models directly on your own computer.

It supports a wide range of open-source AI models and provides an easy-to-use interface for chatting with local models, testing different models, and building AI-powered applications. Because the models can run locally, your conversations and data can remain on your own device without requiring every request to be sent to a cloud AI service.

LM Studio is especially useful for developers and users who want to experiment with local AI agents, coding assistants, offline AI workflows, and private AI applications. It also provides APIs that can be integrated into other software and development projects.

> **⚠️ Storage Reminder:** AI models can take up a significant amount of disk space, especially larger models. If your system drive (usually `C:`) has limited free space, it is recommended to configure LM Studio to store downloaded models on another drive, such as `D:`. This can help prevent your system drive from running out of storage space.
